# HDA Studio Pricing Strategy

## Executive Summary
All prices have been increased by **20%** to create marketing flexibility and maintain profitability when offering promotions or discounts. This strategic pricing allows for:
- **Flash sales** (10-15% off brings prices to near-original levels)
- **Seasonal promotions** without cutting into margins
- **First-time client discounts** to drive acquisition
- **Package deals** with better perceived value

## Marketing Strategies Enabled

### 1. **Promotional Flexibility**
```
Example: "Summer Special - 15% Off All Services!"
- Basic Soft Glam: $90 → $76.50 (still $1.50 above original)
- Standard Glam: $144 → $122.40 (still $2.40 above original)
- Signature Glam: $180 → $153 (still $3 above original)
```

### 2. **First-Time Client Offers**
```
"New Client Welcome: 10% Off Your First Service"
- Brings pricing to ~original levels while maintaining margins
- Drives acquisition without eroding profitability
```

### 3. **Package Deal Perception**
```
"Book 2 Services, Get 20% Off"
- Standard Glam (2x): $288 → $230.40
- Customer saves $57.60
- You still profit over original pricing
```

### 4. **Seasonal Campaigns**
```
"Bridal Season Bonus"
- Bridal Party Package: $300 → $270 (10% off)
- Still maintains original $250 baseline with profit margin
```

## Price Positioning Strategy

### Competitive Analysis Framework

**Entry Level** (Basic Soft Glam - $90)
- Positioned above mass market ($40-60)
- Below luxury studios ($120-150)
- **Value proposition**: Professional quality at accessible pricing

**Mid-Tier** (Soft Glam & Standard Glam - $108-144)
- Competitive with boutique studios
- Premium positioning for special events
- **Value proposition**: Event-ready expertise

**Premium** (Signature Glam - $180)
- Luxury tier pricing
- Competes with high-end editorial artists ($200-300)
- **Value proposition**: Red carpet quality artistry

## Customer Psychology Benefits

### Anchoring Effect
**Original Price Display**: ~~$112~~ **$90** (Basic Soft Glam)
- Shows "retail value" vs "current price"
- Creates perceived discount even at full price
- Builds trust through transparency

### Tiered Value Ladder
1. **$90** - Entry point (Basic Soft Glam)
2. **$108** - Upgrade (+20% more value)
3. **$144** - Premium event tier (+33% from entry)
4. **$180** - Ultimate luxury (+100% from entry)

Each tier offers clear value differentiation.
